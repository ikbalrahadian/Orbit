Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

Our goal is to ensure every customer can successfully install and use Orbit. If you experience a genuine technical problem that prevents you from using the software, we'll work with you to resolve it.

Refunds are available only when Orbit cannot be installed, activated, or used because of a verified technical issue that cannot be resolved by the Developer.

Eligibility

You may request a refund within 30 days of your purchase if:

  • Orbit cannot be installed, activated, or launched.
  • The issue is reproducible and verified by the Developer.
  • The problem cannot be resolved after reasonable troubleshooting.

How to Request a Refund

Please email support@orbitapp.dev within 30 days of purchase.

Your request should include:

  • Your purchase email address.
  • Your order number or receipt.
  • Your Windows version.
  • A description of the issue.
  • Screenshots, error messages, log files, or a screen recording showing the problem.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refund requests may be declined for reasons including, but not limited to:

  • Changing your mind after purchase.
  • Purchasing by mistake.
  • Requesting features not advertised.
  • The software functions as described.
  • Failure to meet personal preferences or workflow expectations.
  • Requests submitted without sufficient supporting evidence.

Refund Process

Each request is reviewed individually. If approved, the refund will be processed through your original payment method. Depending on your payment provider or bank, it may take several business days for the funds to appear.

Need Help?

If you're experiencing a technical issue, we encourage you to contact support before requesting a refund. Many installation and activation issues can be resolved quickly.
