Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbit ("Software") collects, uses, and protects information when you purchase, activate, or use the software.

Orbit is designed to collect only the information necessary to provide licensing, activation, customer support, and product improvements. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Orbit, we may collect:

  • Name and email address provided during purchase.
  • License key and license status.
  • Software version.
  • Windows version.
  • Activation timestamp.
  • Basic device identifier used for license validation.
  • Support emails and attachments you voluntarily send us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver your software license.
  • Verify license activation.
  • Prevent license abuse.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Respond to refund requests.
  • Improve Orbit and fix bugs.

3. Payment Processing

Payments are securely processed through our authorized payment provider (such as Lemon Squeezy).

Orbit does not collect or store your credit card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

4. License Activation

Orbit requires an internet connection during activation to validate your license.

Only the minimum information required for license verification is transmitted.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may only be shared when necessary to:

  • Process payments.
  • Provide software licensing services.
  • Comply with applicable laws or legal obligations.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide licensing, customer support, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

8. Your Rights

Where applicable under local law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

You may also contact us if you have questions about how your data is handled.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
