Support

Need help with Orbit? We're here to help you get back up and running as quickly as possible.

📧 For all support requests, please contact us at hello@airstudio.site. Please include your license email, your Windows version, and a detailed description of the issue to help us assist you faster.

What We Can Help With

Installation

Problems installing or launching Orbit.

License Activation

Activation failures, license verification, or device limits.

License Transfer

Moving your license to a new computer or replacing an old device.

Bug Reports

Unexpected crashes, errors, or software issues.

Feature Requests

Ideas and suggestions for future Orbit releases.

Refund Requests

Assistance with eligible refund requests under our Refund Policy.

Before Contacting Support

  • Make sure you're using the latest version of Orbit.
  • Review the Frequently Asked Questions on the Orbit homepage.
  • Include screenshots or screen recordings if you're reporting a bug.
  • Include any error messages exactly as displayed.
  • For activation issues, include the email address used during purchase.

Support Hours

Support is provided via email on a reasonable-effort basis. Most requests receive a response within 1–3 business days, although response times may vary during weekends and holidays.
